Common Questions
Got questions? We have answers to help you feel more at ease.
How do I book an appointment?
You can easily book an appointment online through our website. Just select your preferred service, choose a date and time, and fill in a few details to secure your spot!
What aftercare should I follow for my tattoo?
After getting your tattoo, it’s essential to keep it clean and moisturized. We recommend using our Redemption or soothing gel products. Avoid sun exposure and soaking in water for at least two weeks. We provide a detailed aftercare guide after your session along with the appropriate products.
Can I customize my tattoo design?
Absolutely! We love personalized designs. Bring your ideas, sketches, or inspiration, and our talented artists will work with you to create something unique that reflects your vision.
What should I expect during my session?
Your session begins with a consultation where we discuss your design and preferences. Then, the artist will begin the tattoo process, and you’ll be able to listen to music or chat to make it a comfortable experience. We want you to feel at ease!
Can I get a piercing during my visit?
Yes, we offer a variety of piercing options. If you’re interested, please let us know when you book your appointment, and we can accommodate both services during your visit.
