Your Questions, Answered

Explore our frequently asked questions to learn about flash tattoos.
What is a flash tattoo?

A flash tattoo is a pre-designed tattoo that’s ready to go — usually part of a curated sheet of designs. Clients choose from these designs instead of creating a custom piece from scratch.

 

Do I need an appointment for a flash tattoo?

Yes! While we do offer walk-ins, booking an appointment ensures your design choice and preferred time are reserved.

Can I modify a flash design?

Minor adjustments like size or small placement tweaks are usually possible, but large alterations may require a custom tattoo booking instead.

How much do flash tattoos cost?

Prices vary by size, placement, and artist. Flash tattoos may have set prices, but your artist will confirm the final cost before starting.

Do you offer flash events or special flash sheets?

Yes! We regularly host themed flash days and artist-curated flash sheets. Follow us on social media to stay updated on upcoming events.

What should I do to prepare for my flash tattoo?

  • Get a good night’s sleep

  • Eat before your appointment

  • Stay hydrated

  • Avoid alcohol or blood thinners 24 hours before your appointment
