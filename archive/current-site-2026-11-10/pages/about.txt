Our Journey
A Brief Look at Our Studio's Evolution
Welcome to our story! From our humble beginnings to becoming a celebrated tattoo shop, our journey has been fueled by passion and creativity. Each milestone marks our dedication to perfecting the art of body modification and a commitment to our community. Join us as we reflect on the highlights that shaped who we are today.
Opened Our Doors
We launched our studio with a vision to offer unique and artistic tattoo experiences. This date marks the beginning of something beautiful in body art.
Expanded Our Services
After receiving an amazing response, we introduced piercings and teeth whitening, providing our clients with even more options for self-expression.
Community Focused
We've evolved into a community-focused business, regularly fundraising for nonprofits like the Breast Cancer Research Foundation, Humane Society, Toys for Tots, and more!
Through the Years
Over the years, we’ve grown, evolved, and embraced new trends, all while staying true to our artistic roots. Our story is just beginning!
