Our Operations Policy
Welcome to Sideshow Productions!
We’re committed to providing a safe, hygienic, and professional environment for all our services. To ensure the best experience for everyone, we kindly ask our clients to adhere to the following policies:
Consent and Identification
Consent Forms:
All clients receiving services must sign a consent form acknowledging understanding and agreement to the procedures.
Valid ID Requirement:
Every client must present a valid government-issued ID. This is crucial for age verification and record-keeping.
Policies for Minors (General Studio Access)
Studio Access:
Minors are only allowed in the studio for
piercing-related services
and must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at all times.
Documentation for Minors (Piercings):
Minors must bring their
birth certificate
and a
photo ID
(valid passport, driver’s license, or school ID). The birth certificate is
mandatory
.
Parent/Guardian ID:
Parents or legal guardians must also provide a valid government-issued ID.
Tattoo Services (18+ Everyday)
Age Restriction (Standard):
We do
not
provide tattoo services to individuals under the age of 18 for regular, day-to-day appointments.
Exception:
See
“Tattoo Services for Minors (16–17) — Promotional Events Only”
below.
Tattoo Services for Minors (16–17) —
Promotional Events Only
To participate, all of the following requirements must be met without exception:
Required Documents (Minor):
Birth certificate
of the child/minor.
Driver’s license
; if none, a
school ID
may be used. (A clear
photo/screenshot
of the ID is acceptable.)
Required Forms (Parents/Guardians):
Both
parents or legal guardians must complete and sign a
Consent to Tattoo
form.
Provide a
copy of both parents’/guardians’ IDs
.
School Portfolio Check:
A parent or legal guardian will be asked to pull up the child’s
school website portfolio
; a
photo or screenshot
will be requested for our records.
Guardian Presence:
A parent or legal guardian must remain
present for the entire duration
(including any waiting period).
If the parent/guardian
leaves for any reason
, the tattoo service will
stop immediately
and the child will need to wait until the
next promotion
to begin or finish the tattoo.
Placement Limits:
Arms and legs only.
When Available:
Tattoos for minors (ages
16–17
) are
only
available during
flash promotions, special events, or fundraisers
.
Promotional designs only
; everyday custom bookings for minors are
not
offered.
Scheduling & Capacity:
Availability is limited and may be first-come, first-served during events. Artist approval is required.
Touch-Ups for Promotional Minor Tattoos:
Touch-ups follow our
standard touch-up policy
(see below) unless otherwise specified by the artist at the event.
Touch-Up Policy
Initial Touch-Ups:
Touch-ups on tattoos are
free within the first 30 days
of the original tattoo.
Additional Touch-Ups:
Any further touch-ups are at the artist’s discretion and may incur additional charges.
Right to Refuse Service
Sideshow Productions reserves the right to refuse service to anyone for any reason. This may include, but is not limited to, client behavior, health conditions, or if the requested service violates our professional standards or ethical guidelines.
Sales Policy
All Sales Final:
Please be advised that all sales are final. We ensure the highest quality in our work and products, and we stand by our services.
Safety and Hygiene
Our studio adheres to strict hygiene and safety protocols. We use
100% disposable, sterilized tubes, needles, and pigments
for each client to ensure your safety and peace of mind.
We appreciate your cooperation and understanding of our policies. These guidelines are in place to ensure the safety, comfort, and satisfaction of all our clients. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of our policies, please reach out to us at
Sideshow Productions
.
